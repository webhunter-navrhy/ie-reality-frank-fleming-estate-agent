**ON VIEW THIS SATURDAY, 3RD OCTOBER, FROM 12.00PM UNTIL 12.40PM**

Frank Fleming Estate Agent is delighted to present to the market No.16 Cliftonville Road. A spacious and extended 3-bedroom period property located right in the heart of Glasnevin. Built by Alexander Strain in the early part of the last century. This handsome bay-windowed redbrick home also benefits from a large attic conversion (currently used as a bedroom) and would appeal to those seeking a family home in a superbly convenient location off Botanic Road.

The accommodation consists of a porch leading to a bright entrance hall, interconnecting formal dining and lounge rooms, the rear of the property is open plan and consists of a large kitchen and dining room. The rear garden is accessible from this room. There is also a convenient guest WC located under the stairs. On the return there is a large family bathroom. The first floor consists of three bedrooms, two large double rooms and a single. A full set of stairs leads up to the large attic room (currently used as bedroom 4) with a dormer window and has its own convenient wc. The front garden is easily maintained with a good selection of mature shrubs and plants. The rear landscaped garden has an array of mature shrubs and plants and is a very private space to entertain. There is also the opportunity to create off-street parking to the rear.

Cliftonville Road is in one of North Dublin’s most prestigious locations. Just minutes’ walk from the National Botanic Gardens, the Bon Secours Hospital and a host of local amenities, including shops, schools and churches. There are also numerous sporting and recreational amenities within the immediate area. Dublin Airport is within a 15-minute drive of the property, as is the M50 motorway which provides easy access to the national road network. The local area is well serviced by bus, railway station at nearby Drumcondra and the LUAS at Phibsboro. Dublin City Centre is within walking distance.
